# Globalisation is a Myth: speaker script (about 3 minutes)

Each `▶` is one click: a new message drops into the group chat.

**0 · Title** (on screen while you walk up)

**1 · Set-up**
"Fifty of us, twenty-three countries, one building in the Czech Republic. ▶ We study together, eat together, even fight for the same washing machine together. ▶ So we are global now… aren't we? Today I'll tell you why I don't think so."

**2 · Proposition**
"My answer is no. Globalisation, the idea that living together turns us into one global culture, is a myth. ▶ Our passports cross borders, sure. But our habits, our food, our music, our hearts… they stay at home."

**3 · Objections**
"I know what you're thinking. We have classes together in English, ▶ we have lunch together, we travel to Vienna together. On paper we look very global. ▶ Isn't that proof? Well… from nine to seven, maybe."

**4 · Evidence 1: the 7:01 PM clock**
"But watch what happens at 7:01 PM. ▶ Class is over, and suddenly English disappears. Spanish over here, Portuguese over there. ▶ And poor Mia is left asking: guys… English?"

**5 · Evidence 2: our stomachs have a flag**
"Second: our stomachs. Lara never goes anywhere without her mate. ▶ João's pão de queijo, rice and beans. Camila's ceviche. ▶ And me? I'd cross the whole city for an arepa. We share a kitchen, not a cuisine."

**6 · Evidence 3: the party test**
"And the final test: the party. International pop plays… polite dancing. ▶ Then Brazilian funk: the Brazilians explode. ▶ Salsa, reggaeton: the Latinos take over the floor. Our energy comes from home."

**7 · Wrap-up**
"So, is globalisation real? I don't think it is. And honestly, that's the best part. We don't become one culture; we bring fifty of them. We always carry our home country in our backpack. ▶ The choice is ours: open it and share it. ▶ That's what makes this place special, isn't it?"
