# What Christlike Leadership Means: speaker script (10 minutes)

Open `/christlike`. Each `▶` is one click: the words and the pencil drawings come in on the click, and the road along the bottom moves on to the next stop; then everything stays still while you speak.
Never more than six words on the screen.

## How to read it

- `▶ [WORDS]`: click; the words in brackets appear on the screen.
- **Pauses** (the same sourced lengths as the seed talk): `/` 1–2 s · `//` 2–3 s · `///` 3–5 s · `(1)` one beat of silence right before a key line.
- **Intonation:** `↗` your voice rises (an item in a list, a question, "there's more coming") · `↘` it falls (a sentence you mean, the end of a list). CAPITALS = stress that word.
- **Rapport:** **we / us / let's** in bold · *question tags and negative questions* in italics.
- **Repetition:** lines marked ⟳ come back. Say them the same way, with the same pause, every time.
- `[CHECK]` = a detail filled in from what you told me. Change it if it didn't happen that way.

## The talk

**0 · Blank paper** *(while you walk up: stand still, smile, look at the people)*

**1 · Why do I want to lead? (1:55)**

▶ [EVERYBODY WANTS TO LEAD, RIGHT?] *(a very happy leader on a pedestal: crown, cape, hands on hips)* Many people want to be a leader, ↗ *right?* ↗ /
Look at him. / So HAPPY. ↘ / So POWERFUL. ↘ //

▶ [SOMETIMES WE EVEN FIGHT FOR IT.] *(a cartoon fight cloud, and the crown flying away)* Sometimes ↗ we even FIGHT ↗ to be the leader! ↘ / *(smile, and wait for the laugh)* //

▶ [WHY DO I WANT TO LEAD?] *(someone thinking, hand on chin)* But let me ask you something. / (1) Why do I want to be a leader? ↗ // Why do YOU? ↗ ///

▶ [FAME AND APPLAUSE?] *(a person on a stage, in the spotlight)* Is it FAME ↗ and applause? ↗ //

▶ [POWER AND CONTROL?] *(someone up high, pulling strings)* POWER ↗ and control? ↗ //

▶ [MONEY AND RICHES?] *(a person hugging a sack of money)* MONEY ↗ and riches? ↘ //
Many leaders want these. / And to get them, ↗ they USE people. ↘ /

▶ [WHO CAN LIFT ME?] *(people standing on people, a crown at the top)* Their real question is: / (1) "Who can lift ME?" ↘ ///

▶ [OR SOMETHING GREATER?] Or… / do we want something GREATER ↗ than all of these? ↗ ///

▶ [WHAT CHRISTLIKE LEADERSHIP MEANS] Today, ↗ let's talk about that something greater: / (1) what Christlike leadership MEANS. ↘ //

**2 · What leadership really is (90 s)**

▶ [WHAT LEADERSHIP REALLY IS] *(a road draws itself along the bottom, with people along it)* Leadership is not a ladder. ↘ / It's a ROAD. ↘ / A long road, ↗ with people all along the way. ↘ //

▶ [EVERY DAY, AN OPPORTUNITY] Every one of **us** starts a leadership journey. ↘ / It starts at home, ↗ at school, ↗ at work. ↘ / And every day, ↗ the road gives **us** an OPPORTUNITY to lead. ↘ //

▶ [IT SHALL NOT BE SO] Jesus didn't lead from a throne. ↘ / He led on the ROAD. ↘ / And He told His disciples: / (1) "It shall not be so among you. ↘ / Whosoever will be chief among you, ↗ let him be your SERVANT." ↘ ///

▶ [WHO CAN I LIFT?] *(one hand reaches down, another reaches up)* So a Christlike leader asks a different question: / (1) "Who can I LIFT?" ↘ ⟳ ///
Let me show you my road. ↘ //

**3 · My road (1:40)**

▶ [VENEZUELA · THE WORST TIME.] My road went through Venezuela. / (1) The WORST time of Venezuela… ↘ //

▶ [THE WORST TIME. THE BEST TIME.] …was the BEST time of Venezuela. ↘ ⟳ ///

▶ [CARACAS, 2016 · THREE MEALS: A PRIVILEGE] *(the road turns orange up to the first stop)* In 2016 I was eighteen, ↗ a missionary in Caracas. ↘ /
In many homes, ↗ and at times in mine, ↗ three meals a day were a PRIVILEGE. ↘ //

▶ [I GAVE MY DINNER.] *(second stop)* Many nights, ↗ I gave my dinner ↗ to grandparents and children in the street. ↘ / (1) Another opportunity to lead. ↘ ⟳ //

▶ [TWO HOURS. FOUR. EIGHT. TWELVE.] *(third stop)* Then the lights started to go out. / Two hours a day. ↗ / Then four. ↗ / Then eight. ↗ // Sometimes… (1) TWELVE. ↘ ///

▶ [SO WE LIT CANDLES.] So **we** lit candles. ↘ / And as missionaries and church members, ↗ **we** noticed who was in need, ↗ and **we** helped. ↘ / (1) Another opportunity to lead. ↘ ⟳ //

**4 · What the road taught me (2:15)**

▶ [ONE · SHARE THE LITTLE] That road taught me three things ↗ about Christlike leadership. ↘ //
ONE: share the little you have. ↘ / Jesus fed five thousand people ↗ with one boy's lunch: ↗ five loaves ↗ and two small fish. ↘ / The boy gave what he had, ↗ and it was ENOUGH. ↘ //

▶ [TWO · LOVE IN ACTION] TWO: lead with love in action. ↘ / Not with control. ↘ / With love, ↗ example ↗ and patience. ↘ //

▶ [HE WASHED THEIR FEET.] *Isn't that what Jesus did?* ↗ / On His last night, ↗ He knelt down ↗ and washed His disciples' FEET. ↘ / Then He said: / "I have given you an EXAMPLE." ↘ //
"We love Him, ↗ because He first loved us." ↘ ///

▶ [THREE · LEADERSHIP WITH A SMALL L] THREE: Christlike leadership is leadership with a small L. ↘ / No title. ↘ / No money. ↘ / No fame. ↘ /
Kim B. Clark called it "the kind of leadership that builds ↗ and lifts ↗ and inspires." ↘ / On my road, ↗ nobody was the boss. / But everybody was leading. ↘ //

▶ [ONE CANDLE LIGHTS ANOTHER] Like a candle. / One candle can light another… ↗ / (1) and it loses NOTHING. ↘ ///
A Christlike leader doesn't make followers. ↘ / They make new LEADERS. ↘ //

**5 · The best time (30 s)**

▶ [PEOPLE BECAME THE LIGHT.] So why was it the best time? ↗ / Because when the lights went out, ↗ (1) people became the LIGHT. ↘ //

▶ [VENEZUELA · THE BEST TIME.] The worst time of Venezuela… ↘ / was the BEST time of Venezuela. ↘ ⟳ ///

**6 · Back to the road (1:35)**

▶ [EVERY STOP, AN OPPORTUNITY] *(point at the road)* Look at this road again. / Every stop was an opportunity to lead. ↘ / And none of them came with a title. ↘ //

▶ [MADE FOR THIS.] Fame fades. ↘ / Power ends. ↘ / Money runs out. ↘ //
But leading like Christ… ↗ (1) that's what we were MADE for. ↘ / Even when we were hungry, ↗ it made us feel FULL. ↘ / And it's what people REMEMBER. ↘ ///

▶ [WHO CAN I LIFT?] Your road continues tomorrow. ↘ / So every morning, ↗ ask: / (1) "Who can I LIFT?" ↘ ⟳ ///

▶ [WHAT KIND OF LEADER?] Imagine if every leader followed Jesus. / Every parent. ↗ Every teacher. ↗ Every boss. ↘ // What would the world be like? ↗ ///
So… / what kind of leader do YOU want to be? ↗ ///
**Let's** walk this road together. ↘

▶ [THANK YOU.] Thank you.

## Before you practise

- **Practise with a timer.** At a calm 120 words a minute, with every pause, the script runs about 9:30 (9:10 a little faster, 10:00 a little slower), right at the limit of the 10 minutes. If you need room, the first lines to drop are "Isn't that what Jesus did?" and "Kim B. Clark called it…". If you run long, cut words, never pauses.
- On every click the words write themselves in and the drawings trace themselves. Click, let it arrive, then speak.
- The road along the bottom appears at "What leadership really is" and turns orange up to each stop of your story: hunger, sharing, the dark. The rest of the road stays ahead, unwalked: that part is the audience's. At "Every stop, an opportunity", point at it.
- Don't read the screen. The words are short so the audience takes them in at a glance while you look at the audience.
- The refrains ⟳ are your anchors: "Who can I lift?", "Another opportunity to lead" and "the worst time… the best time". If you get lost, go to the next one.
- "Who can lift ME?" and "Who can I LIFT?" use the same words in a different order. Stress ME the first time and LIFT the second, so the audience hears the switch.
- At "What kind of leader do YOU want to be?" stop for a full three seconds.
- The English is kept simple on purpose: short sentences, no local words to explain. The only old-style English is in the scriptures; say those slowly.

## Where the facts come from (if someone asks)

- **How hungry the country was (not on a slide any more, in case someone asks):** ENCOVI 2017, the national living-conditions survey: 61% went to bed hungry, 64% lost 11 kg on average. Reported by [El Espectador](https://www.elespectador.com/mundo/mas-paises/el-64-de-venezolanos-perdio-11-kilos-por-falta-de-alimentos-article-740425/) and [La Patilla](https://lapatilla.com/2018/02/21/encuesta-revela-que-64-de-venezolanos-perdio-11-kilos-por-falta-de-alimentos/).
- **Three meals a day:** keep it about the homes you saw, as written. The surveys say about a third of people ate two meals or fewer, not most.
- **March 2019 blackout** (four to six days depending on the source, about 20 million people without water): [Mongabay](https://es.mongabay.com/2019/04/venezuela-emergencia-apagon/).
- **"Leadership with a small L":** Kim B. Clark, then president of BYU-Idaho, commencement address of 14 December 2007: [transcript](https://www2.byui.edu/Presentations/Transcripts/Graduation/2007_12_14_Clark.htm). It is also in the BYU-Pathway lesson on disciple leadership.
- **Scriptures:** "it shall not be so among you… let him be your servant", Matthew 20:25–27 · five loaves and two fish, John 6:9 · Jesus washes the disciples' feet, John 13:1–5 · "I have given you an example", John 13:15 · "We love him, because he first loved us", 1 John 4:19.
