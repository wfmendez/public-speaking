# What Christlike Leadership Means: speaker script (10 minutes)

Open `/christlike`. Each `▶` is one click: the words, the drawings and the candles change on the click, then everything stays still while you speak.
Never more than six words on the screen.

## How to read it

- `▶ [WORDS]`: click; the words in brackets appear on the screen.
- **Pauses** (the same sourced lengths as the seed talk): `/` 1–2 s · `//` 2–3 s · `///` 3–5 s · `(1)` one beat of silence right before a key line.
- **Intonation:** `↗` your voice rises (an item in a list, a question, "there's more coming") · `↘` it falls (a sentence you mean, the end of a list). CAPITALS = stress that word.
- **Rapport:** **we / us / let's** in bold · *question tags and negative questions* in italics.
- **Repetition:** lines marked ⟳ come back. Say them the same way, with the same pause, every time.
- `[CHECK]` = a detail filled in from what you told me. Change it if it didn't happen that way.

## The talk

**0 · Title** *(black while you walk up)*

▶ [WHAT CHRISTLIKE LEADERSHIP MEANS] *(the figure of Christ draws itself in light; stand still and look at the people)* ///

**1 · The paradox (30 s)**

▶ [VENEZUELA · THE WORST TIME.] (1) The WORST time of Venezuela… ↘ //

▶ [THE WORST TIME. THE BEST TIME.] …was the BEST time of Venezuela. ↘ ⟳ ///
Sounds strange, *doesn't it?* ↗ / How can a time of hunger ↗ be the best time? ↘ //

**2 · Hunger (45 s)**

▶ [CARACAS, 2016 · THREE MEALS: A PRIVILEGE] In 2016 I was eighteen, ↗ a missionary in Caracas. ↘ /
In many homes, ↗ and at times in mine, ↗ three meals a day were a PRIVILEGE. ↘ //

▶ [2017 · SIX IN TEN SLEPT HUNGRY] By 2017, six out of ten Venezuelans ↗ went to bed HUNGRY. ↘ //

▶ [PUMPKIN AREPAS. MANGO DINNERS.] **We** made our arepas with pumpkin, ↗ so the flour would last. ↘ /
And in mango season, ↗ people ate three, ↗ four mangoes, ↗ just to quiet the hunger. ↘ //

**3 · The queues (45 s)**

▶ [WE QUEUED BY ID NUMBER] **We** could only buy food on the day our ID number allowed. ↘ / Hours in the sun. ↘ /

▶ ["THE FLOUR IS HERE!"] Then a message in the group chat: / "The flour is here!" ↗ / And everyone RAN. ↘ //

▶ [SAME QUEUE. TWO KINDS OF PEOPLE.] In those queues, ↗ some people argued. ↘ / Others brought chairs, ↗ talked, ↗ even studied together. ↘ //
Same queue. / Two kinds of people. ↘ ///

**4 · The blackouts (60 s)**

▶ [TWO HOURS. FOUR. EIGHT. TWELVE.] Then the lights started to go out. / At first, two hours a day. ↗ / Then four. ↗ / Then eight. ↗ // Sometimes… (1) TWELVE. ↘ ///
And with the light went the WATER. ↘ / And with the water went our PATIENCE. ↘ //

▶ [2019 · DAYS IN THE DARK] In March 2019, ↗ the whole country went dark for days. ↘ /
I was studying in Cúcuta, ↗ on the other side of a closed border, ↗ while my family was in the dark. ↘ //

▶ [SO WE LIT CANDLES.] *(the candle lights; wait for it)* So **we** lit candles. ↘ ///
But in that dark, ↗ I saw the BEST of my people. ↘ / And those candles taught me three things about leadership. ↘ //

**5 · One: share the little (55 s)**

▶ [ONE · SHARE THE LITTLE] ONE: a Christlike leader shares the little they have. ↘ /
When you have little, ↗ you want to keep it. ↘ / I saw the opposite. //
On my mission, many nights I gave my dinner to grandparents and children in the street. / I wasn't a hero. ↘ / Everyone was doing it. ↘ //

▶ [FIVE LOAVES. TWO FISH.] Jesus didn't feed five thousand people from a full table. / He started with one boy's lunch: ↗ five loaves ↗ and two small fish. ↘ /
The boy gave what he had, ↗ and it was ENOUGH. ↘ //

▶ [STRETCH THE FLOUR. STRETCH OUR LOVE.] **We** learned to stretch the flour… ↘ / and **we** learned to stretch our LOVE. ↘ ///

**6 · Two: hope, even when hungry (50 s)**

▶ [TWO · HOPE, EVEN WHEN HUNGRY] TWO: a Christlike leader gives hope, ↗ even when they're hungry too. ↘ /
As a missionary ↗ and as church members, ↗ **we** noticed the empty fridges. ↘ /
**We** couldn't always fill them, ↗ but **we** could visit, ↗ listen, ↗ and leave a little HOPE. ↘ [CHECK] //

▶ [HE WASHED THEIR FEET.] *Isn't that what Jesus did?* ↗ / On His last night, ↗ He knelt down ↗ and washed His disciples' FEET. ↘ //
Serving is not about how much you have. ↘ / It's about who you kneel for. ↘ ///

**7 · Three: no money, no title (50 s)**

▶ [THREE · NO MONEY. NO TITLE.] THREE: you don't need money or a title to lead. ↘ /
Remember that message, ↗ "The flour is here!"? ↗ / Whoever sent it could have kept quiet ↗ and gone first. ↘ / They told everyone. ↘ //

▶ [NOBODY WAS THE BOSS.] Nobody was the boss. / But everybody was leading. ↘ //

▶ [ONE CANDLE LIGHTS ANOTHER] *(a second candle lights)* That's what a candle does. / One candle can light another… ↗ //

▶ [IT LOSES NOTHING.] (1) …and it loses NOTHING. ↘ ///

**8 · The best time (55 s)**

▶ [PEOPLE BECAME THE LIGHT.] *(many candles light)* Why the best time? ↗ / Because when the lights went out, ↗ (1) people became the LIGHT. ↘ //
And it hasn't stopped. / This June, ↗ two earthquakes hit Caracas and La Guaira. ↘ /
I went out to help, ↗ and the streets were full of cars and motorbikes ↗ carrying food. ↘ /

▶ [2026 · EVERY FIFTEEN MINUTES, SOMEONE NEW.] At a building that had fallen, ↗ **we** received the donations. /
Every FIFTEEN minutes, ↗ someone new arrived, ↗ with whatever they had. ↘ //

▶ [VENEZUELA · THE BEST TIME.] The worst time of Venezuela… ↘ / was the BEST time of Venezuela. ↘ ⟳ ///

**9 · Your candle (60 s)**

▶ [LET YOUR LIGHT SO SHINE] *(the figure of Christ comes back, behind the candles)* In Matthew five, Jesus said: /
"Let your light so shine before men, ↗ that they may see your good works." ↘ //

▶ [NOT A SPOTLIGHT. A CANDLE.] So this is what Christlike leadership means. ↘ / It's not a spotlight. ↘ / It's a CANDLE. ↘ //

▶ [WHO IS IN THE DARK?] Who is in the dark ↗ in your home, ↗ in your class, ↗ in this academy? ↘ //
You don't need a crisis to lead like Christ, *do you?* ↗ / You only need a little light, ↗ and the courage to share it. ↘ //
**Let's** light one candle this week. ↘ / *Wouldn't that change this place?* ↗ ///

▶ [THANK YOU.] Thank you.

## Before you practise

- **Practise with a timer.** At a calm 120 words a minute, with every pause, the script runs about 8:50 (8:25 a little faster, 9:15 a little slower), which leaves room inside the 10 minutes. If you run long, cut words, never pauses.
- On every click the words come up one by one and the drawings trace themselves; at the start of each part the screen comes up from black. Click, let it arrive, then speak.
- At "So we lit candles", "One candle lights another" and "People became the light" the candles take a second or two to light.
- Don't read the screen. The words are short so the audience takes them in at a glance while you look at the audience.
- The refrain ⟳ is your anchor: if you get lost, go to the next refrain.

## Where the facts come from (if someone asks)

- **Six in ten went to bed hungry:** ENCOVI 2017, the national living-conditions survey (61% went to bed hungry, 64% lost 11 kg on average). Reported by [El Espectador](https://www.elespectador.com/mundo/mas-paises/el-64-de-venezolanos-perdio-11-kilos-por-falta-de-alimentos-article-740425/) and [La Patilla](https://lapatilla.com/2018/02/21/encuesta-revela-que-64-de-venezolanos-perdio-11-kilos-por-falta-de-alimentos/).
- **Three meals a day:** keep it about the homes you saw, as written. The surveys say about a third of people ate two meals or fewer, not most.
- **Mangoes against hunger (2016):** [CBC / Reuters](https://www.cbc.ca/1.3620964).
- **Queues by ID number (2016):** [The World](https://theworld.org/stories/2016/07/30/attention-venezuelan-shoppers-will-id-card-keep-you-loyal-revolution).
- **March 2019 blackout** (four to six days depending on the source, about 20 million people without water): [Mongabay](https://es.mongabay.com/2019/04/venezuela-emergencia-apagon/). The bridges at Cúcuta had closed on 22–23 February: [MPR / AP](https://www.mprnews.org/story/2019/02/23/soldiers-unleash-tear-gas-tension-venezuela-border).
- **The June 2026 earthquakes** (magnitude 7.2 and 7.5, mainly Caracas and La Guaira): [Telemundo](https://www.telemundo.com/noticias/noticias-telemundo/desastres-naturales/live-blog/dos-terremotos-magnitud-superior-7-sacuden-venezuela-segundos-rcna351682), and the World Food Programme on neighbours handing out food and water: [La Patilla](https://lapatilla.com/2026/08/19/a-dos-meses-del-doble-terremoto-el-programa-mundial-de-alimentos-atiende-a-mas-de-300-mil-personas-en-venezuela/).
- **Scriptures:** five loaves and two fish, John 6:9 · Jesus washes the disciples' feet, John 13:1–5 · "Let your light so shine", Matthew 5:16.
