# Everyday AI Agents: Class Storyline (20 min with the video)

The video asks **"…who decides?"** and answers **"You do."** The class takes that answer and proves it with **one real story: yours**, building the Cumorah Academy Party game with an agent. Every concept appears *inside* that story, never as a loose list.

**Main idea (the one sentence they should remember):** *An agent can do the work, but you're the DJ: you choose the goal and you check the result.*

**The thread running through the whole class:** the DJ. In the video it's a hint, in Act 1 it's a metaphor, in Act 3 it's a warning, and at the end it's the answer.

## The twist (decided)
The agent said *"Done ✓, tests: 12 / 12 passing"*. When 20 students joined at once, the game froze: the tests only checked a single player. You told the agent, it fixed it, and that time it did work. Lesson: **"Done" doesn't mean "right". You test, you decide.**
