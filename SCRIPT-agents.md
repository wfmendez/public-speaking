### 2 · Chatbot talks. Agent does.
`[CLICK]`
A chatbot talks. You ask a question, it gives you an answer, and the work is still yours.
`[CLICK]`
An agent does. You give it a goal, and it searches, opens your calendar, writes the email, and comes back when it's done.
That is the whole class in one sentence: **a chatbot answers; an agent acts.**

### 3 · Agent = DJ. Tools = tracks.
`[CLICK]`
Here is an easy way to remember it. Think of a DJ.
A DJ doesn't write every song. A DJ chooses the right track at the right moment and mixes them.
An agent is the same. It doesn't do everything by itself. It uses tools:
`[CLICK]` search the web,
`[CLICK]` check a calendar,
`[CLICK]` send an email,
`[CLICK]` write and run code.
The skill of an agent is choosing and mixing the right tools for your goal.

> *Nota:* Cada clic sube un fader y añade una capa real a la música (hats, bajo, melodía, beat). Deja que se escuche un segundo antes de seguir: la clase *oye* la metáfora.

### 4 · Read the crowd. Adjust the mix.
`[CLICK]`
And a good DJ does one more thing: they watch the crowd. If people stop dancing, the DJ changes the song.
Agents do this too. After every action they look at the result: did it work? If not, they change the plan and try again.
This "look and adjust" is what makes an agent an agent. Remember it, because now we're going inside one.

## Part 2 · Inside an agent (5:00)

### 5 · Inside an agent
`[CLICK]` *(camera flies into the 3D world; let it land)*
Welcome inside an agent. In the middle is its "brain", the AI model. Around it is a loop, and the white light travels around it again and again. Every agent works with these four steps.

### 6 · The agent loop
`[CLICK]` *(the loop from above)* Let's visit each step.
`[CLICK]` **Goal.** Everything starts with what you want. "Plan my weekend trip to Prague under 150 euros."
`[CLICK]` **Plan.** The agent breaks the goal into small steps: find trains, compare hostels, check the weather, build a schedule.
`[CLICK]` **Act.** It uses its tools to do each step. It actually searches, actually compares.
`[CLICK]` **Check.** It looks at the result. Is it under 150 euros? Is the hostel near the station? If not, back to Plan.
Goal, plan, act, check, and again, until the goal is done. *(point at the moving light)* That light never stops.

> *Nota:* La cámara viaja a cada nodo con cada clic. Deja que llegue antes de hablar.

### 7 · Tools are its hands
`[CLICK]`
Without tools, an AI can only talk. Tools are its hands.
`[CLICK]` Search, to find information.
`[CLICK]` Calendar, to see your time.
`[CLICK]` Email, to talk to other people.
`[CLICK]` Files, to read and save documents.
`[CLICK]` Code, to build and calculate.
An agent is only as useful as the tools you give it, and only as safe as the permissions you give it.

### 8 · Memory keeps the context
`[CLICK]`
The last piece is memory. Without memory, every conversation starts from zero.
`[CLICK]` It remembers **you**: your name, your role.
`[CLICK]` It remembers the **history**: what it already tried, what worked, what failed.
`[CLICK]` And your **preferences**: "I prefer trains to buses", "I'm vegetarian."
Memory is why the second time you ask, the answer is better.

### 9 · Chatbot vs agent
`[CLICK]`
So let's compare. A chatbot answers once: one question, one answer, done.
`[CLICK]`
An agent loops until done: goal, plan, act, check, as many times as it needs.

## Part 4 · Using agents well (16:30)

### 12 · You stay in charge
`[CLICK]` *(each rule types itself on its own click)*
Agents are powerful, but you stay in charge. Three rules:
`[CLICK]` **Give a clear goal.** "Help me" is a bad goal. "Find three hostels in Prague under 30 euros near the station" is a good goal.
`[CLICK]` **Check the work.** Agents make mistakes. They can sound sure and still be wrong. Always review before you send, pay, or submit.
`[CLICK]` **Protect private data.** Never give an agent your passwords, bank details or private information it doesn't need. Give it the smallest permissions that work.

### 13 · You are the DJ
`[CLICK]` *(the music drops in at full volume, then let it play two seconds)*
So remember the DJ. The agent can mix the tracks, read the crowd and change the plan.
But you choose the music. You decide the goal, you check the result, you say when it's good.
**You are the DJ.**
